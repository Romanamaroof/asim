Islamabad, The Beautiful

Islamabad, the capital of Pakistan,
is located on the Pothohar Plateau in northern Pakistan, approximately 14 km northeast of Rawalpindi, forming a metropolitan area of over 5.7 million people Wikipedia Wikipedia +1 . Established in the 1960s and officially designated as the capital in 1967, Islamabad was designed to replace Karachi as the national capital due to strategic, climatic, and administrative considerations Wikipedia Wikipedia +1 . The city covers about 906 square kilometers and is divided into eight zones, including administrative, diplomatic, residential, educational, industrial, commercial, rural, and green areas
